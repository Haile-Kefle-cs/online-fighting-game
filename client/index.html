📖 How to Play

🎯 Game Objective

Eliminate all enemy players and be the last soldier standing! Work with your team to defeat the opposing team.

🎮 Controls

  • ⬆ ⬇ ⬅ ➡ or W A S D - Move your soldier
  • Space or Click - Shoot your weapon
  • J - Light Attack (melee)
  • K - Heavy Attack (melee)
  • L - Special Attack
  • E - Pickup weapon from the ground
  • Tap/Click on screen to aim and shoot

🔫 Weapons

  • 🔫 Pistol - Starting weapon, balanced damage
  • 🔫 Rifle - Medium damage, good range
  • 💥 Shotgun - High damage, short range
  • 🎯 Sniper - Very high damage, long range
  • 🚀 Rocket - Massive damage, explosive
  • ⚡ Laser - Fast fire rate, consistent damage

👥 Teams

  • 🔴 Red Team - Fight for the red side
  • 🔵 Blue Team - Fight for the blue side
  • Teams are automatically balanced when players join
  • You cannot damage your own team members

💡 Tips & Strategy

  • 💪 Build combos by hitting enemies consecutively for bonus damage
  • 🔫 Pick up weapons from the map for better firepower
  • 🛡️ Use melee attacks when enemies are close
  • 🎯 Aim carefully with sniper and rocket launcher
  • 👥 Stick with your team for better survival
  • 📱 Mobile users: Use the on-screen touch controls
